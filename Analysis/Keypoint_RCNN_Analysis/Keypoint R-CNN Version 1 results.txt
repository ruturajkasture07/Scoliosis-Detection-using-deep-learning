## Analysis of Version 1 Performance

Looking at your Version 1 results, I can see several clear patterns that will guide our optimization for Version 2:

### Key Observations from V1 Training:

**1. Convergence Speed**
- Loss dropped dramatically in early epochs (0.6831 → 0.0997 in first 10 epochs)
- Best validation loss at epoch 26 (0.0617) but plateau started around epoch 22
- Model didn't fully converge within 30 epochs according to convergence analysis

**2. Precision vs Recall Trade-off**
- Precision is consistently high (92-94% after epoch 10)
- Recall is relatively low (47-52% range)
- This suggests the model is conservative - when it predicts a keypoint, it's usually right, but it misses many keypoints

**3. F1 Score Plateau**
- Best F1 was 0.6678 at epoch 4 (early peak)
- F1 fluctuated between 0.60-0.67 throughout training
- No sustained improvement in F1 after epoch 10

**4. MAE Progress**
- Val MAE improved from 0.2198 to 0.0366 (good improvement)
- Still has room for improvement for medical applications

### Optimization Strategy for Version 2:

Based on this analysis, here's what we need to address:

1. **Improve Recall** - We need the model to find more vertebrae keypoints
2. **Better Convergence** - Need to train longer or adjust learning rate schedule
3. **Stabilize F1 Score** - Reduce fluctuations in later epochs
4. **Reduce Overfitting Signs** - Val loss started increasing while train loss continued decreasing after epoch 26
